1.4 Need for the Project

The development of FloraLens is motivated by the clear requirement for a unified digital platform capable of handling both tangible product sales and intangible calendar-scheduled service appointments. In tier-2 cities like Kolhapur, Sangli, and Pune, customers planning private celebrations currently navigate multiple fragmented vendors: visiting a physical wholesale market for loose stems, contacting an independent decorator via phone calls, negotiating rates without written contracts, and coordinating manual payments.

For vendors, the absence of accessible digital management tools forces reliance on physical paper ledgers. This operational friction results in accidental double-bookings on auspicious wedding dates (muhurats), lost customer communications, and severe financial losses when customized floral inventory goes unsold. FloraLens fulfills this structural need through automated inventory decrement, transparent package itemization, and deterministic slot conflict detection.

1.5 Scope of the Project

The architectural scope of FloraLens is defined by the following functional boundaries:

• Multi-Tier Flower Retail Catalog: Dynamic product listings categorized by floral variety (Roses, Lilies, Orchids, Carnations) featuring multi-tiered stem variants (Standard, Deluxe, Premium).

• Event Decoration Booking Engine: Category-based service discovery (Weddings, Birthdays, Anniversaries, Baby Showers) with package tiers (Silver, Gold, Diamond), customizable add-on accessories, and date/time-slot selection.

• Double-Booking Prevention Engine: Rigorous server-side collision validation ensuring no vendor can be booked twice for overlapping time intervals on the same calendar date.

• India-Centric Commerce & Localization: Real-time Indian Rupee (INR) financial math, structured Indian postal address management (pincode, city, state, landmark), and Razorpay sandbox simulation.

• Role-Based Portals: Independent dashboard environments for Customers, Vendors, and Platform Administrators, protected by role-based routing guards.

1.6 Target User Personas

1. Retail Customers: Individuals purchasing bouquets for gifting or booking decorated venues for intimate family events with upfront pricing and verified delivery timelines.

2. Independent Florists & Decorators: Small business owners seeking digital showcase capabilities, streamlined order fulfillment progression, and schedule management.

3. Platform Administrators: Governance operators auditing transactions, reviewing vendor KYC documentation, and moderating marketplace product catalogs.
