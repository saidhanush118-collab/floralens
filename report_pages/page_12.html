2.3 Existing Online E-Commerce Aggregators

Several commercial platforms cater to portions of the gifting and event planning landscape:

• National Gifting Portals (e.g., Ferns N Petals, Floweraura): These platforms excel at cross-city floral delivery and standardized gift combos. However, they operate primarily via centralized franchise hubs rather than empowering local neighborhood florists. Their catalogs feature rigid pre-packaged combos with limited transparency regarding individual florist provenance. Crucially, they do not offer comprehensive on-site event venue decoration with crew scheduling.

• Home Service Platforms (e.g., Urban Company): While offering on-demand home cleaning, salon services, and basic festive balloon decoration, these platforms employ closed gig-worker models rather than open vendor marketplaces. Customers cannot select a specific trusted local florist or inspect custom wedding mandap portfolios.

• Event Directory Aggregators (e.g., WedMeGood, WeddingWire): These services serve primarily as digital classified directories. They list decorator phone numbers and portfolio photographs but lack real-time transactional checkout, live calendar slot validation, and transparent pricing.

2.4 Limitations of Existing Systems

A comprehensive review of the current market highlights four primary architectural and functional limitations:

1. Siloed Architecture: No mainstream platform unifies both perishable flower delivery and high-value venue decoration under one cohesive transactional basket.

2. Schedule Double-Booking Vulnerabilities: Existing directory systems allow customers to request bookings on dates when the decorator is already committed, resulting in last-minute vendor rejections or unqualified subcontractor outsourcing.

3. Lack of Granular Vendor Controls: Local florists lack lightweight self-service portals to update real-time stem inventories, adjust prices for festive peaks, or configure specific delivery radius zones.

4. Inflexible Package Customization: Conventional carts do not allow customers to combine fixed base packages (e.g., Gold Mandap) with arbitrary add-on accessories (e.g., dry ice fog entry, fairylight backdrops).
