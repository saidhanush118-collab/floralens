9.4 Current Limitations

The current version operates in a local/cloud development environment with simulated SMS/WhatsApp OTP alerts and mock Razorpay credentials. In addition, delivery logistics rely on florist self-dispatch rather than automated third-party hyper-local courier integration (e.g., Dunzo or Porter).

9.5 Future Scope and Feature Roadmap

Future development of the FloraLens platform will focus on advanced technical expansions:

1. AI-Powered Floral & Theme Recommendation Engine: Integrating collaborative filtering algorithms to recommend complementary floral arrangements and decorative color palettes based on event type, venue dimensions, and seasonal flower availability.

2. Augmented Reality (AR) Venue Preview: Utilizing WebXR to allow customers to superimpose 3D models of wedding mandaps, floral arches, and balloon backdrops onto live video feeds of their physical venues.

3. Hyper-Local Courier API Integration: Connecting automated webhooks to on-demand logistics partners (Shadowfax, Porter, Dunzo) for live GPS delivery tracking and optimized multi-drop routing.

4. Milestone Escrow Payment Contracts: Implementing multi-stage escrow release for high-value wedding decorations (e.g., 20% advance booking, 50% on material arrival, 30% on inspection sign-off).

5. IoT Cold-Chain Telemetry: Deploying BLE temperature and humidity sensor tags in bulk floral transit boxes to monitor freshness conditions and trigger automated compensation if thresholds are breached.

6. Direct Farm-to-Florist Wholesale B2B Marketplace: Expanding the platform upstream to connect local retail florists directly with commercial polyhouse flower cultivators in Talegaon and Pune, bypassing mandi middlemen.
