7. FIELD WORK AND DATA COLLECTION

7.1 Nature of the Field Project Work

In accordance with the B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering field project guidelines, our field investigation focused on the practical observation, workflow analysis, and data modeling of independent floral retailers and event decoration agencies in the Kolhapur metropolitan area and neighboring commercial corridors (Shahupuri, Laxmipuri flower market, and Rajarampuri).

The field study was structured around three core investigative activities:

1. Retail Florist Operational Workflow Observation: Examining daily stem procurement cycles from wholesale mandis, water-retention practices, bouquet assembly durations, and retail wastage rates.

2. Event Decorator Scheduling Audit: Interviewing independent decorators regarding calendar management, booking lead times (typically 3 to 14 days), advance payment policies, and recurring schedule conflicts during peak wedding seasons.

3. Consumer Purchase Habit Survey: Documenting consumer grievances concerning lack of price transparency, inconsistent stem quality, and difficulty in finding trustworthy decorators for domestic functions.

7.2 Requirement Gathering and Stakeholder Needs

The field study revealed specific stakeholder requirements that guided system design:

• Florist Needs: Need for rapid inventory updates, protection against uncommitted orders, and localized delivery radius controls to prevent stem wilting during transit.

• Decorator Needs: Critical requirement to prevent double-bookings, itemized package agreements (specifying backdrop dimensions, floral densities, and lighting addons), and structured milestone tracking.

• Customer Needs: Upfront, all-inclusive pricing in INR, clear photographs of realistic past work rather than generic stock imagery, and transparent delivery slot guarantees.
